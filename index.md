## About
Hi, I’m **Jayden Jung**, a student at **Brea Olinda High School** in California.  
I’m interested in **AI-driven economy**, **game theory**, and **multi-agent learning**, especially how cooperation and competition (co-opetition) emerge when agents learn over time.

## Current Focus
- Replicator-guided warm-start reinforcement learning for faster convergence to game-theoretic equilibria  
- Modeling research collaboration as a co-opetition system using public consortium participation data  
- Steering frozen generative models with black-box oracles they were never trained against  
- Building clean, reproducible simulation pipelines and evaluation metrics

## Featured Projects

**Replicator-Guided Warm-Start for Co-opetition Learning**  
I combine evolutionary game theory (replicator dynamics) with multi-agent reinforcement learning to show that two learning processes can converge to the same equilibrium, but with very different convergence speeds depending on initialization.
[Read more](/research/coopetition/) · [Code](https://github.com/jaydenjung-bohs09/Multi-Agent-Reinforcement-Learning-Through-Game-Theoretic-Replicator-Dynamics)

**ESAP Gen AI × Bio: An End-to-End In Silico Pipeline for Kinase Lead Discovery**  
A three-stage pipeline built at the Engineering Summer Academy at Penn: predict a protein property from sequence, steer a frozen generative model toward stronger JAK2 binders using physics-based docking as the only feedback, then validate with experimental PDB evidence and an independent structure predictor. The organizing rule is that the model which generates is never the model which grades.
[Read more](/research/esap/) · [Code](https://github.com/jaydenjung-bohs09/ESAP)
